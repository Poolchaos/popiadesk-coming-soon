The eight conditions, in plain terms

Most of the Act's duties sit under these eight. A few have sections of their own, such as appointing an Information Officer, direct marketing and sending information abroad.

  1. 1 Take responsibility Your business is responsible for following all of these, even when someone else handles the information for you. POPIA: Accountability (section 8)
  2. 2 Collect only what you need, lawfully Collect only what you need, for a lawful reason, and get it from the person themselves where you can. POPIA: Processing limitation (sections 9 to 12)
  3. 3 Know why, and don't keep it too long Decide why you need the information before you collect it, and keep it only as long as you need it. POPIA: Purpose specification (sections 13 and 14)
  4. 4 Don't reuse it for something unrelated Don't use information for something new that doesn't fit the reason you collected it for. POPIA: Further processing limitation (section 15)
  5. 5 Keep it correct Keep the information complete, correct and up to date. POPIA: Information quality (section 16)
  6. 6 Be open about it Keep a record of how you use information, and tell people what you collect and why. POPIA: Openness (sections 17 and 18)
  7. 7 Keep it safe Protect information from loss and misuse, and tell the Regulator and the people affected if it is breached. POPIA: Security safeguards (sections 19 to 22)
  8. 8 Let people see and correct it People can ask to see, correct or delete the information you hold about them, and you must answer. POPIA: Data subject participation (sections 23 to 25)
